## Q1. Why was Project Maven created and what was the original goal?

### Takeaway
Deputy Secretary Robert Work created the AWCFT on April 26, 2017 to speed DoD's adoption of big data and machine learning. Its first narrow task was computer-vision processing, exploitation and dissemination (PED) of drone full-motion video, used against ISIS. Lt Gen Jack Shanahan oversaw it and Marine Col. Drew Cukor ran it day to day. The first algorithm was fielded within about 8 months.

### Cited Findings
- **2017-04-26**: DepSecDef Robert O. Work signed the memo "Establishment of an Algorithmic Warfare Cross-Functional Team (Project Maven)" to "accelerate DoD's integration of big data and machine learning". — [NSArchive, scan of the memo](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/4164306/Department-of-Defense-Establishment-of.pdf); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Maven)
- The memo's first task was PED of full-motion video from tactical and medium-altitude UAS. — [GlobalSecurity](https://www.globalsecurity.org/intell/systems/maven.htm)
- The goal was to use deep learning to extract insights from intelligence data and support the fight to defeat ISIS. — [Trajectory Magazine, "Operationalizing Project Maven"](https://trajectorymagazine.com/?p=2565)
- **Funding**: about $70M, received from Congress within roughly two months of creation (2017). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Maven); [GlobalSecurity](https://www.globalsecurity.org/intell/systems/maven.htm). (Search summaries did not give per-year budget lines for FY2018-FY2022; see Gaps.)
- **Leadership**: Lt Gen Jack Shanahan directed Maven from April 2017 to December 2018, then became the first JAIC director. Marine Col. Drew Cukor was chief of the AWCFT and gave much of the day-to-day direction. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Maven); [GlobalSecurity](https://www.globalsecurity.org/intell/systems/maven.htm)
- **2017-12**: The first mission-ready product deployed to the Middle East: an object-recognition algorithm for ScanEagle drone video. — [Trajectory Magazine](https://trajectorymagazine.com/?p=2565); [TechSparx, July 2017, plan to deploy algorithms "by year's end"](https://techsparx.com/blog/2017/07/project-maven.html)
- **Early vendors**:
  - Google worked through prime contractor ECS Federal rather than a direct DoD contract. Google's share was reported at about $9M, though cloud tiering could have made it larger. — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/the-pentagons-controversial-drone-ai-imaging-projectex-1826046321); [ITWire](https://itwire.com/government-tech-policy/83054-pentagon-drone-project-to-earn-google-big-bucks-report.html)
  - ECS later held 3 Maven-related contracts worth $364M from 2017 onward. Clarifai was paid more than $25M as an ECS subcontractor, including for "Development of Facial Recognition". Microsoft (about $30M, from about 2019) and AWS (about $20M, from about 2020) received ECS subcontracts. These subcontracts do not name Maven explicitly; the link is Tech Inquiry's inference. — [ITPro](https://itpro.com/business-strategy/public-sector/360824/amazon-microsoft-google-project-maven-dod-contracts); [Jack Poulson / Tech Inquiry](https://jackpoulson.substack.com/p/budget-confirms-tech-inquirys-reporting)
  - Leaked emails from September 2017 show Amazon was also competing for Maven work. — [ITPro](https://itpro.com/business-strategy/public-sector/360824/amazon-microsoft-google-project-maven-dod-contracts)
- **Google revolt (2018)**:
  - The petition asking Google to cancel the contract and avoid future military work had "nearly 4,000" signatures per Gizmodo. Other reports say 4,600 or more, and Jacobin, a later advocacy-leaning account, says nearly 5,000.
  - About a dozen employees resigned (Gizmodo, May 2018), the first known mass resignations at Google over a business decision. Other outlets said at least 13, and Axios said "dozens".
  - A group of more than 700 "Maven Conscientious Objectors" organized internally.
  - Sources: [Gizmodo AU, May 2018](https://www.gizmodo.com.au/2018/05/google-employees-resign-in-protest-against-pentagon-contract/); [collectiveaction.tech](https://collectiveaction.tech/?p=964)
- **About June 1, 2018**: Google Cloud CEO Diane Greene told staff Google would not seek a follow-on contract when the current one expired in March 2019. — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/military/google-halt-controversial-project-aiding-pentagon-drones-n879471); [Arab News/AFP](https://www.arabnews.com/node/1314041/media); [Silicon UK](https://www.silicon.co.uk/e-innovation/google-end-ai-drone-project-233385)
- **Book (March 2026)**: Bloomberg's Katrina Manson published *Project Maven: A Marine Colonel, His Team, and the Dawn of AI Warfare* (W. W. Norton; audiobook released Mar 24, 2026; hardback edition later in May 2026 per listings). The "Marine Colonel" in the title is presumably Cukor, but no search result confirmed this. — [NPR, Mar 23, 2026](https://www.npr.org/2026/03/23/nx-s1-5757478/inside-a-secret-pentagon-effort-to-bring-ai-to-the-battlefield); [Audible](https://www.audible.com/author/Katrina-Manson/B003X07CK0)

### Inferences
- Maven was meant as a pathfinder: a small, fast team proving DoD could field commercial AI in months. The narrow FMV mission was a means to that end, which explains its later broadening into data fusion and C2.
- Google's exit did not slow the program. ECS, Clarifai, Microsoft, AWS and later Palantir and others filled the gap, and the controversy shaped later DoD AI-ethics work.

### Gaps
- No primary-source FY2018-FY2022 Maven budget line was found; figures such as "$131M FY2018" or "about $250M FY2020" circulate but were not verified here.
- Palantir's role before 2022 (Palantir reportedly took over Google's work after 2019) and Anduril's role were not confirmed by any result.
- Early ISIS operational use outside the ScanEagle deployment, and the Somali drone-footage test mentioned by GlobalSecurity, are uncorroborated.

## Q2. How did governance and ownership move between OSD, JAIC, CDAO, NGA and Army?

### Takeaway
Maven began under OUSD(Intelligence) and stayed there even after the JAIC was created in 2018, although Shanahan moved to lead the JAIC. The FY2023 budget (2022) split it: GEOINT computer vision went to NGA, which made "NGA Maven" a program of record in November 2023, and the non-GEOINT, C2-oriented Maven Smart System went to CDAO. The Army is the contracting agent for MSS. A Feinberg memo of March 9, 2026 then moved MSS oversight from NGA to a new CDAO MSS Program Office and routed all MSS contracts through the Army Enterprise Agreement, with program-of-record status due by the end of FY2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Dec 2018**: Shanahan left Maven to become the first JAIC director. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Maven)
- **FY2023 request (2022)**:
  - The request designated parts of Maven to move from OUSD(I&S) to CDAO and NGA, with the non-GEOINT portions going to CDAO. — [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/?p=18520)
  - NGA said it would take "operational control of Project Maven's GEOINT AI services from the Office of Undersecretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security." — [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/?p=18520)
- **2022-10-07**: The transition was held up by a continuing resolution, and the Pentagon stayed "mum". — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2022/10/07/pentagon-remains-mum-on-project-maven-as-transition-is-held-up-by-continuing-resolution/)
- **2023-11-02 announcement, effective 2023-11-07**: NGA formally designated "NGA Maven" a program of record after about 9 months of requirements work, using the Software Acquisition Pathway. — [Defense Daily](https://defensedaily.com/project-maven-transitions-to-program-of-record-as-nga-looks-to-leverage-program-for-other-uses/intelligence-community); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Maven)
- **2024-03-14 (FY2025 budget books)**: Maven funding was realigned under "PE 0606135D8Z CDAO Activities". The Deputy CDAO said CDAO passed the entire Maven "AI development pipeline" to NGA. NGA, CDAO and OUSD(I&S) were "largely tight-lipped" about Maven and the MSS since the transition. — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2024/03/14/project-maven-fiscal-2025-budget-still-evolving/)
- A Palantir release, quoted by DefenseScoop, describes Maven as "the cloud infrastructure, software capabilities, and AI that powers CDAO's CJADC2 initiatives." — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/?p=86457)
- **NATO distinction (April 2025)**: SHAPE stressed that MSS NATO "should not be confused with the NGA Maven Warfighter Support System." This confirms two parallel lines: NGA Maven (GEOINT AI and warfighter support) and the Palantir MSS. — [SHAPE](https://shape.nato.int/news-archive/2025/nato-acquires-aienabled-warfighting-system.aspx)
- **2026-03-09 Feinberg memo**:
  - MSS is to become a formal program of record before FY2026 ends (Sept 30, 2026).
  - USD(R&E) and USD(I&S) are to steer the "transition of MSS system administration, oversight, support activities, and related responsibilities from NGA to the CDAO MSS Program Office" within 30 days.
  - All MSS contracts move to "the existing Army Enterprise Agreement (EA) contract vehicle".
  - USD(R&E) takes over from NGA as authorizing official for the associated commercial cloud.
  - Sources: [DefenseScoop, Apr 3, 2026](https://defensescoop.com/2026/04/03/palantir-maven-feinberg-directive/); [DefenseScoop, Apr 15, 2026](https://defensescoop.com/2026/04/15/palantir-maven-smart-system-pentagon-program-transition-feinberg/); [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/what-maven-smart-system-and-what-does-it-do)
  - **Date conflict**: DefenseScoop and CSIS give March 9, 2026. Reuters reported the letter around Mar 20-23, 2026, and the advocacy timeline Capture Cascade says Mar 21. These are likely reporting dates, not the signing date. — [GovConWire](https://www.govconwire.com/articles/pentagon-palantir-maven-ai-program-of-record); [Capture Cascade](https://capturecascade.org/event/2026-03-21--palantir-maven-program-of-record)
- **2026-04-03**: The directive makes AI-enabled decision-making "the cornerstone" for CJADC2. — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/04/03/palantir-maven-feinberg-directive/)
- **2026-04-15**: DoD components face an "aggressive" transition timeline, and experts said the "full implications of this major transition remain unclear." — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/04/15/palantir-maven-smart-system-pentagon-program-transition-feinberg/)
- **2026-08-05**: Army Col. Molly Solsbury was appointed MSS program director inside CDAO. The ai.mil bio URL says "Melissa", and she came from the Office of the Army Chief of Staff. The aim is to make MSS a core C2 asset. — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/08/05/pentagon-appoints-new-maven-smart-system-program-director/); [ai.mil bio](https://www.ai.mil/About/Leadership/Bio-Page/Article/4453518/colonel-melissa-solsbury/)
- **Key people**:
  - NGA Director VADM Frank Whitworth (June 2022 to Nov 2025 per Wikipedia; not verified with a primary source).
  - CDAO Cameron Stanley (2026). — [ExecutiveGov](https://www.executivegov.com/?p=289425)
  - DUSD(R&E) James Mazol (2026). — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/09/22/maven-smart-system-ai-james-mazol-cameron-stanley-defensetalks/)
  - Wikipedia's naming of Rachel Martin as Maven director reflects 2024-era information. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Maven)

### Inferences
- The path was OUSD(I) pathfinder (2017-2022), then a split (FY2023) into NGA Maven, a GEOINT program of record from Nov 2023, and MSS under CDAO with NGA administering it and the Army contracting. From March 2026 MSS consolidates under a CDAO MSS Program Office (within R&E), with contracts through the Army EA. Note that the CDAO now sits under USD(R&E) per the Solsbury bio.
- The JAIC was never Maven's owner. It was a parallel organization that took Maven's first leader, and both later merged into CDAO (2022).

### Gaps
- No primary DoD or CDAO release confirming that MSS was actually designated a program of record by Sept 30, 2026 was found. Motley Fool (Aug 25, 2026) and Crypto Briefing say it "is now" one, while other outlets still describe a transition. — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/25/palantirs-maven-is-now-an-official-pentagon-progra/); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/palantir-maven-pentagon-program-of-record/)
- The exact date the CDAO was realigned under USD(R&E) was not researched.
- Names of the CDAO heads between 2022 and 2025 (Craig Martell, then Doug Matty) were not verified in this session.
- The name of Whitworth's successor as NGA director was not found.

## Q3. How did Palantir become the MSS prime, and what are the contract values and dates?

### Takeaway
After a limited-operator prototype, the Army awarded Palantir a $480M, five-year IDIQ in May 2024. In May 2025 the ceiling rose by $795M to about $1.3B through 2029. The Army's $10B, 10-year enterprise agreement (summer 2025) became the vehicle for all MSS contracts under the March 2026 memo. NATO bought its own MSS NATO in March 2025. FY2027 budget documents request $1.5B to $2.3B, depending on scope.

### Cited Findings
- **2024-05-29**: The Army awarded Palantir a $480M firm-fixed-price contract for the "Maven Smart System prototype", with estimated completion on May 28, 2029. It expands access to thousands of users at CENTCOM, EUCOM, INDOPACOM, NORTHCOM and TRANSCOM, plus the Joint Staff. Palantir had been developing the prototype "with a limited number of operators." MSS ingests satellite imagery, geolocation and other data to detect potential targets, and supports CJADC2. — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2024/05/29/palantir-480-million-army-contract-maven-smart-system-artificial-intelligence); [Defense News/C4ISRNet](https://www.defensenews.com/artificial-intelligence/2024/05/30/palantir-wins-contract-to-expand-access-to-project-maven-ai-tools/); [MeriTalk](https://www.meritalk.com/articles/army-awards-480m-contract-for-maven-prototype/)
- **Sole-source basis**: The Pentagon certified Palantir as the only supplier for MSS (Tech Inquiry reporting). — [Jack Poulson](https://jackpoulson.substack.com/p/pentagon-certified-palantir-as-only)
- **2025-03-25 (finalized) and 2025-04-14 (announced)**:
  - NCIA and Palantir finalized the acquisition of "MSS NATO" for Allied Command Operations (SHAPE). The value was not disclosed, and procurement took about 6 months from requirement to contract.
  - Uses: intelligence fusion, targeting, battlespace awareness and planning. It includes LLMs and ML, and SHAPE plans to add new AI models and modeling and simulation tools.
  - Sources: [NCIA](https://www.ncia.nato.int/newsroom/news/nato-acquires-aienabled-warfighting-system); [SHAPE](https://shape.nato.int/news-archive/2025/nato-acquires-aienabled-warfighting-system.aspx); [The Defense Post, Apr 16, 2025](https://www.thedefensepost.com/2025/04/16/nato-palantir-ai-warfighting-platform)
- **2025-05-23**: DoD raised the ceiling by $795M to "nearly $1.3B" through 2029, anticipating a "significant influx in demand". An Army official said no acquisition decisions had been made yet, and the ceiling is a cap, not committed funding. — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2025/05/23/dod-palantir-maven-smart-system-contract-increase/)
  - Figure note: $480M + $795M = about $1.275B, reported as "nearly $1.3B". The user's "~$795M ceiling" is the increment, not a ceiling.
- **2025-07/08 (Palantir announced about Aug 1, 2025)**:
  - The Army signed a 10-year Enterprise Agreement (EA) with Palantir worth up to $10B. It consolidates 75 contracts (15 prime and 60 related).
  - The Army says $10B is a maximum, "not any specific obligations."
  - Date conflict: Washington Technology dates it August 2025, while some sources say July 31, 2025.
  - Sources: [Washington Technology](https://washingtontechnology.com/contracts/2025/08/palantir-signs-10b-enterprise-agreement-army/407153); [HSToday](https://www.hstoday.us/industry/industry-news/u-s-army-awards-palantir-potential-10-billion-contract/); [TechNews.tw, Aug 1, 2025](https://finance.technews.tw/2025/08/01/palantir-lands-enterprise-deal-worth-up-to-10b-from-u-s-army/)
- **2025-09-10**: MSS was extended to the Marine Corps. — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2025/09/10/palantir-maven-smart-system-mss-marine-corps/)
- **2026-03-09**: All MSS contracts are to move to the Army EA vehicle (see Q2). — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/04/03/palantir-maven-feinberg-directive/)
- **FY2027 request (DefenseScoop, 2026-05-28)**:
  - More than $1.5B to expand access to MSS under the "Joint Force AI-Enabled Headquarters initiative", within more than $2B for CJADC2 and Mission Command Applications. — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/05/28/dod-fy27-budget-cjadc2-maven-smart-system-palantir/)
  - The DoD FY2027 Budget Overview and Greenberg Traurig give $2.3B for "Maven Smart System and Joint Fires Network". — [FY2027 Overview Book](https://comptroller.war.gov/Portals/45/Documents/defbudget/FY2027/FY2027_Budget_Request_Overview_Book.pdf); [Greenberg Traurig](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2026/5/understanding-the-presidents-fy-2027-budget-request-for-the-department-of-war)
  - Other outlets describe the $2.3B as covering 5 years. — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/palantir-maven-pentagon-program-of-record/); [ISS Tracker](https://isstracker.pl/en/news/pentagon-poszukuje-23-miliarda-dolarow-na-system-pola-bitwy-maven-ai,x4x5P)
  - **Conflict**: these figures measure different scopes, and possibly a single year versus the FYDP (5-year plan). Not reconciled.
- **Unverified**: an Aug 2026 memo reportedly directing up to $244M in extra Palantir funding through March 2027 comes from an aggregator (KuCoin) only. — [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/palantir-surpasses-1-billion-in-u-s-government-contracts-in-2026)

### Inferences
- Palantir's path to prime ran: incumbent prototype developer (about 2022-2024), then sole-source certification, then the $480M IDIQ, then the ceiling raise, then enterprise-agreement consolidation. Each step increased lock-in, and the March 2026 memo made the Army EA the single vehicle.
- Contract ceilings ($1.3B, $10B) far exceed reported obligations. Treat them as caps, not revenue.

### Gaps
- No primary-source evidence was found for the start date of Palantir's pre-2024 MSS prototype work (about 2022-2023?).
- No Palantir SEC filing language on MSS revenue was retrieved.
- The value of the NATO MSS contract was not disclosed.
- No specific Golden Dome role for MSS was found in any source; only the CJADC2 and Joint Fires Network roles are documented.

## Q4. What is the status as of 2026 (to Oct 2026)?

### Takeaway
By autumn 2026, MSS was DoD's de facto AI-enabled C2 and targeting backbone. It reached more than 100,000 users (up from about 50,000 in January 2026 and more than 20,000 in May 2025), was used heavily in Operation Epic Fury against Iran (13,000 targets in 38 days, per the CDAO), and was being pushed to all commands and the National Guard Bureau. It now sits under a CDAO program office headed by Col. Solsbury, and program-of-record status was due by Sept 30, 2026, though no official confirmation was found. LLM integration, notably Anthropic's Claude, is contested after DoD's March 2026 supply-chain-risk designation of Anthropic.

### Cited Findings
- **May 2025 (GEOINT 2025)**: NGA Director Whitworth said Maven was available to all services and combatant commands, with more than 20,000 active users across more than 35 tools in 3 security domains. Users had more than quadrupled since March 2024, and model latency improved 80% in the year after program-of-record status. — [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/05/ai-unchained-ngas-maven-tool-significantly-decreasing-time-to-targeting-agency-chief-says/); [ECS](https://ecstech.com/artificial-intelligence/geospatial-intelligence/the-maven-program); [Defense One, May 2025](https://defenseone.com/technology/2025/05/ai-helping-pentagon-go-finding-targets-predicting-threats/405279)
  - Alternate 2025 metrics: more than 130 sites and about 25,000 personnel (Hvylya citing Wired; secondary source). — [Hvylya](https://en.hvylya.net/news/1529-130-sites-1-billion-detections-the-silent-global-spread-of-america-s-ai-war-machine)
- **2026-09-22 (DefenseTalks)**:
  - DUSD(R&E) James Mazol said MSS users went from about 50,000 in January 2026 to more than 100,000 after Operation Epic Fury began, and that MSS is being pushed to all commands and the National Guard Bureau.
  - CDAO Cameron Stanley said Maven helped strike 13,000 targets in 38 days during Epic Fury, and that MSS is expanding into logistics, supply chain, readiness and budgeting data.
  - Sources: [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/09/22/maven-smart-system-ai-james-mazol-cameron-stanley-defensetalks/); [The Defense Post, Sept 25, 2026](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/09/25/pentagon-maven-ai-expansion/amp/)
- **Sept 2026**: Defense One says Maven is "becoming the Pentagon's everything app", having replaced "six, eight, ten" legacy systems according to the CDAO. — [Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/technology/2026/09/maven-becoming-pentagons-everything-app/415882/)
- **Iran / Operation Epic Fury (from Feb 28, 2026)**:
  - US officials said the Pentagon relied on Maven to identify top-priority targets and help choose weapons. — [Arms Control Association, May 2026](https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2026-05/news/ai-plays-major-role-war-iran)
